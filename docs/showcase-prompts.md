# Showcase prompts: real jobs, one sentence at a time

Each workflow starts with **Generate** (text to image) and then runs a chain of **Edit** steps on the result. Every step uses default settings (Balanced). Each step edits the previous step's output unless it says *from the original*.

They are chosen to show what professionals actually pay for: product variants, localization, virtual staging, campaign versions, and text that stays sharp.

---

## 1. E-commerce: one product shot → a full catalogue

**Who:** online shops, Etsy and Amazon sellers.
**Why it matters:** one studio photo becomes every colour variant and a lifestyle shot, without a reshoot.

| Step | Mode | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Generate · 1:1 | A matte ceramic coffee mug on a seamless white studio background, soft box lighting from the left, gentle shadow, product photography, sharp focus |
| 2 | Edit | Change the mug colour to sage green, keep the lighting and shadow identical |
| 3 | Edit *from the original* | Change the mug colour to terracotta, keep the lighting and shadow identical |
| 4 | Edit | Place the mug on a wooden kitchen counter in warm morning light, with a blurred window behind it |
| 5 | Edit | Print the word "MONDAY" in small black serif letters on the side of the mug |
| 6 | Expand · 4:5 | Continue the kitchen counter and the window (Instagram portrait crop) |

## 2. Marketing: localize a poster without a designer

**Who:** agencies, event organisers, anyone launching in more than one country.
**Why it matters:** text in the image is translated in place, and the layout, font style and photo stay the same.

| Step | Mode | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Generate · 2:3 | A minimalist poster for a jazz festival: a golden saxophone on a deep navy background, large bold white title "BLUE NIGHTS JAZZ FESTIVAL", below it "12–14 JULY · RIVERSIDE PARK", clean modern typography |
| 2 | Edit | Translate all the text into Italian: "FESTIVAL JAZZ NOTTI BLU" and "12–14 LUGLIO · PARCO SUL FIUME", keep the same font style and layout |
| 3 | Edit *from the original* | Translate all the text into German: "BLUE NIGHTS JAZZFESTIVAL" and "12.–14. JULI · UFERPARK", keep the same font style and layout |
| 4 | Edit *from the original* | Change the dates to "9–11 AUGUST" and add a small line "SOLD OUT" diagonally in red in the top right corner |
| 5 | Expand · 16:9 | Continue the navy background (website banner version) |

## 10. Cleanup jobs nobody wants to do by hand

**Who:** photographers, real estate, product listings.
**Why it matters:** the boring retouching work, done in one sentence each.

| Step | Mode | Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Generate · 3:2 | A street-level photo of a charming bakery storefront with a striped awning, but with parked cars, a trash bin and tangled power cables in front of it |
| 2 | Edit | Remove the parked cars, the trash bin and the power cables, keep the storefront unchanged |
| 3 | Edit | Change the shop sign to read "Forno Lucia — since 1962" |
| 4 | Edit | Make the sky clear blue and the light bright and cheerful |

---

### Tips when running these

- Keep **"keep … the same"** in colour and material changes. It tells the model what must not move.
- For text, put the exact words in quotes. Short phrases render most reliably.
- *From the original* steps are variants. Running them all from the same base keeps the series consistent.
- If a result is close but not right, try the same prompt with a new seed before rewording it.
