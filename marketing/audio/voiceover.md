# Voiceover script — v2 (approved 2026-10-10)

Tone: direct and plain-spoken, a little cheeky, confident. Short sentences, one idea each: the
app is yours and it stays on your PC. The Hook's "yours" comes back in the Outro. Times are the
scene windows in the current cut; the redesign times the scenes to these lines.

| # | Scene | Window | Line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intro (the mark) | 0:00–0:03.4 | Introducing Kokon. |
| 2 | Hook (wall of edits) | 0:03.4–0:07.8 | Another image editor? Nope. This one is yours. |
| 3 | Create | 0:07.8–0:20.2 | Kokon runs on your PC, and everything stays there. Describe an image, and your graphics card makes it. No cloud. No account. No subscription. |
| 4 | Region | 0:20.2–0:38.2 | Want to change just one thing? Paint over it, and say what you want. Remove the scooter. Add a cat. Everything else stays exactly as it was. |
| 5 | Versions | 0:38.2–0:42.6 | Changed your mind? Every version is saved. |
| 6 | Montage | 0:42.6–1:00.7 | Change the season. Fix the sign. Restyle a portrait. Swap the furniture. Expand the frame. Shoot a product ad. One sentence each. |
| 7 | Agent | 1:00.7–1:09.4 | Use an AI agent? Kokon works with it too. Just ask for an image. |
| 8 | Hardware | 1:09.4–1:16.8 | Windows or Mac. NVIDIA, AMD, Intel or Apple silicon. It runs on what you have. |
| 9 | Outro | 1:16.8–1:24.7 | Kokon. Free, open source, and yours. Try it now. Your PC already knows what to do. |

About 130 words. The take is generated with `gen_voice.py final` (voice, seeds and pauses per
line are in FINAL there); files land in voice/final/, one per line, mastered to -16 LUFS.
